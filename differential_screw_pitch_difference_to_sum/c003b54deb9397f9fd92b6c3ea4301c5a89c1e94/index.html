两份独立提示词

True

A stepped screw has two same-handed helical threads with different pitches. Its coarse section passes through a fixed supported nut; its fine section passes through a coloured output nut on an anti-rotation carriage. The input wheel smoothly turns through about one revolution and stops. A fixed ruler lies next to the carriage. The screw advances through the fixed nut while the output carriage moves only a small distance, equal to the difference of the two thread pitches per revolution.

Counterfactual

A stepped screw has two same-handed helical threads with different pitches. Its coarse section passes through a fixed supported nut; its fine section passes through a coloured output nut on an anti-rotation carriage. The input wheel smoothly turns through about one revolution and stops. A fixed ruler lies next to the carriage. The screw advances normally through the fixed nut, but the unchanged fine-thread output nut anomalously advances the carriage much farther: its travel equals the sum of the two same-handed thread pitches per revolution.
