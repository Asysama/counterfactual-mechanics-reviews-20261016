A stepped screw has two same-handed helical threads with different pitches. Its coarse section passes through a fixed supported nut; its fine section passes through a coloured output nut on an anti-rotation carriage. The input wheel smoothly turns through about one revolution and stops. A fixed ruler lies next to the carriage. The screw advances through the fixed nut while the output carriage moves only a small distance, equal to the difference of the two thread pitches per revolution.
