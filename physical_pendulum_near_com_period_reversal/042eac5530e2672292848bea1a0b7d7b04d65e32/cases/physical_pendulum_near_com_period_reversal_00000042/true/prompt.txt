Two identical rigid rods hang from fixed horizontal axles on a stand. Dark stripes mark their centres of mass. The left axle is 0.30 rod lengths above its centre; the right axle is only 0.085 rod lengths above its centre. Both rods start at the same small angle, and their latches withdraw together. Both rods swing freely under gravity. The right rod, pivoted close to its centre of mass, oscillates more slowly and has a longer period than the left rod.
